1. Set the mission

40 seconds
Active Cases - DATA view; do not click yet.

SSA receives wage information from beneficiaries, employers, and authoritative feeds. When those sources do not agree, an analyst must determine what happened, document the evidence, and route the case to the right outcome.

Dynamics 365 Customer Service supplies the case lifecycle, Dataverse supplies the connected data, and Power Platform supplies the automation and reporting. Together they turn that review into one structured, auditable process without building a new case-management platform.

Dynamics feature paired to the story

Customer Service case managementThe discrepancy becomes a governed work item with an owner, priority, status, activities, and resolution.
Power Platform foundationDataverse, Power Automate, Power BI, and Copilot can extend the same case without creating another data silo.
SSA configures its process on a proven platform instead of building case management from scratch.

2:40to 4:10

4. Trace discrepancy to evidence

90 seconds
Show Hargrove Q1 and Q2 discrepancy records, then the evidence records.

The quantitative core of the review is stored as structured discrepancy data. For Q1 and Q2 of 2025, Hargrove reported zero dollars. The authoritative wage source shows 12,400 dollars in each quarter, producing the 24,800-dollar total.

Dataverse relationships connect each quarter and employer to the parent case. Because these are secured records rather than text in a note, SSA can aggregate exposure, filter by employer or period, validate data, and trigger downstream automation.

The evidence package is structured the same way. The W-2, employer verification, beneficiary statement, and wage-feed match each carry a type, source reference, description, and verification status. Power Automate can create tasks or notifications when evidence is missing, while the reviewer can see exactly what supports the conclusion and what still needs attention.

4:10to 5:45

5. Move from analyst finding to supervisor decision

95 seconds
Open the Hargrove finding, then the Supervisor Case Summary Report V2.

The analyst's formal work product is the Investigation Finding. It combines a finding type, recommended disposition, analyst identity, and narrative details in one reviewable Dataverse record.

For the supervisor, the embedded case summary brings the key facts onto one page: beneficiary context, discrepancy totals, evidence readiness, analyst conclusion, and the proposed decision. The supervisor does not need to reconstruct the case from emails, attachments, and separate systems.

Dynamics can surface that summary directly on the case form, route an approval task, and notify the supervisor in the tools they already use. Copilot can later summarize the case and ground a draft narrative in the same approved records. The supervisor still owns the decision: approve the recommendation or return the case for more work.

75 seconds

The Nguyen case shows the completed lifecycle. The evidence was reviewed, the finding was documented, supervisor approval changed to Yes, and the standard case-resolution process captured an explicit disposition.

Dynamics audit history preserves who changed the decision, when it changed, and its previous value. Field security, record ownership, and retention policies protect the record throughout its lifecycle. If OIG, program leadership, or an authorized reviewer asks how SSA reached the outcome, the answer is attached to the case.

Operational charts can show cases by stage, risk, disposition, and pending approval, while Power BI can provide program-level trends from the same Dataverse data. What you have seen is one connected process: intake, triage, discrepancy analysis, evidence, finding, supervisor decision, and resolution.
