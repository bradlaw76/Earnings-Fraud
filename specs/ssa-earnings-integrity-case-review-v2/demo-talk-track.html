Why Dynamics 365 Customer Service
3 minPlatform
▼
Narrative
Before opening the app, explain why D365 Customer Service — not a custom-built tool — is the right platform. This positions every subsequent screen with platform justification rather than just feature demonstration.
Actions
  • Stay on the app selector or a blank tab. Walk through D365 capabilities verbally before clicking into anything.
  • Open Earnings Integrity V2 Demo App. Land on Active Cases – DATA. Say: "This is the intake queue. Every earnings discrepancy referral lands here."
  • Point out the OOB view columns: Title, Customer (beneficiary), Priority, Status Reason. "Zero custom development for this grid — this is D365 out of the box."
  • Show the filter/sort bar. "An analyst or supervisor can sort by risk, filter by disposition, or group by stage — no custom code, no BI report."
Built-in D365 CS capabilities leveraged by this solution
Case (incident) entity — OOB work item with lifecycle, timeline, activities, and SLA. No custom case table needed.
Queues & routing — cases land in priority queues, assigned to analysts or teams without custom routing logic.
Activities & timeline — calls, tasks, emails, and notes attach to the case automatically. Full interaction history, zero configuration.
Business Process Flow — guided stage-by-stage progression from intake to resolution. Required fields enforced at each stage gate.
Dataverse relationships — discrepancy records, evidence items, and findings link to the case with cascade-delete integrity and no custom FK logic.
Web resources — HTML/JS reports embedded in case forms. The Supervisor Summary Report V2 is a web resource registered in this app (confirmed: 2 components, type 62).
Model-driven app — security roles, sitemap, forms, and views configured in Power Apps. No custom UI framework or hosting required.
Copilot for D365 CS — case summarization, suggested responses, and knowledge search available in the same workspace (future activation — the slot is already there).
Key Points
"SSA does not need to build a case management system. It needs to configure one. The infrastructure — queues, routing, timeline, audit log, BPF — is D365 out of the box."
The custom work in this solution is the domain vocabulary: discrepancy types, evidence categories, finding dispositions. Everything else is inherited from the platform.

Supervisor Approval as a boolean is a deliberate design choice: binary, explicit, timestamped, and immutable once set. Not a comment field — a decision field.
"Every field on this form can be required at a specific BPF stage. You cannot advance the case to Resolution without Risk Rating populated. The system enforces the process."
